# Third-party data and redistribution

The shared bundle includes only the **open** catalogue export.

- PackSense India / Open Food Facts data: ODbL 1.0.
- Open Prices: ODbL 1.0.
- AGMARKNET metadata/reference: Government Open Data License - India.

The database is an adapted catalogue. If it is publicly redistributed or used
to provide a public derived database, comply with ODbL attribution and
share-alike requirements. Preserve this notice and
`provenance/source_manifest.json`.

BigBasket, DMart, Zepto, and JioMart snapshots are excluded from this shared
bundle because their dataset redistribution terms are unclear. The private
internal catalogue and all raw source dumps are also excluded.

Runtime model weights are not bundled:

- PaddleOCR models are obtained by the PaddleOCR package.
- `BAAI/bge-reranker-v2-m3` is obtained from Hugging Face and remains subject
  to its model-card licence and terms.
- Optional `microsoft/trocr-base-handwritten` is obtained from Hugging Face.
